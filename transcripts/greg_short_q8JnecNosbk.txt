If I was 17, I would probably ask myself two things. One, what beliefs do I have because the people around me told me to have those beliefs? So am I going to college because my parents told me to go to college? Do I eat a certain way because they told me to eat a certain way? Am I chasing a certain career path because they told me that that's what I should be doing? So at that age, I didn't have much free agency. I had a lot of frustration because I was told what to do and it didn't feel right, but I wasn't thinking for myself. So at 17, start thinking for yourself and preparing yourself to develop free agency, which is difficult at your age. But if you're asking this question, I bet you can handle it.
