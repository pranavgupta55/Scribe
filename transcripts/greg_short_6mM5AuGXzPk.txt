Today, building for volume is one of the greatest opportunities that exists. The lesson isn't that cheap products are better than more expensive products. The lesson is that you should create a product that solves a massive amount of people's problems that a massive amount of people want to buy. Why would you build a product for only a thousand people if you can build a product for 10 million people, or in our case, over a billion people drink energy drinks? Again, you could start niche, but as you're thinking about the vision for your grand career, for your grand magnum opus of what you're going to create in your life, think big. And that doesn't mean think expensive products. And most of people I know, maybe they just happen to be in my network and my circle, that have become outrageously successful, have built businesses where they are selling product or solving problems for ordinary people with ordinary spending habits. Products that people actually use and actually tend to tell their friends about.
