My name is Greg Lavecki. I am the co-founder of Bloom, which is a multi-hundred million dollar brand. I am 30 years old, and I'm here to tell you the difference between selling a product and building a brand that people will line up down the street for. Building a brand is all about the community. You have to over-invest. Every extra dollar that you have is not going to go towards meta-ads trying to go convert consumers and sell more product, but when you reinvest in your brand, you are throwing events for your community. You are creating top-performing podcasts with just invaluable free information for all of your community to learn from. You are investing in making your product better and better and innovating it every single year. Do not just always put every dollar back into more Facebook ads and back into more performance marketing. That is how you will slowly just create a product that is selling, but you will hit a ceiling. If your goal here is to create something that is actually going to move culture and be a brand that has household awareness across all of America, when you were just at your Thanksgiving table, you could have brought it up and people would recognize what you were talking about, that's a brand. You are going to need to double down and invest in your community. That is how you do something culturally special. That is how you build a brand and not just sell a product.
