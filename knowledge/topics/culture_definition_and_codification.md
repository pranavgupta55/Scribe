## Claims
- Culture is the rules (spoken and unspoken) that govern reinforcement in an organization. A rule is an if-then: 'If you do X, then you get rewarded/punished.' — [watch_this_to_get_ahead_in_2026_spXH1kJ6q-Q.txt]
- Culture is hard to transpose between orgs because there are 100+ rules that must be observed over time; codify by chunking rules into 3 values that unpack to the rules. — [watch_this_to_get_ahead_in_2026_spXH1kJ6q-Q.txt]
- Example rule at Acquisition.com: if you're not talking in a meeting, you're muted; not paying attention is grounds for termination. — [watch_this_to_get_ahead_in_2026_spXH1kJ6q-Q.txt]
- When expanding to new locations, move your biggest culture champion there first, but ensure culture can self-reinforce without that leader. — [watch_this_to_get_ahead_in_2026_spXH1kJ6q-Q.txt]
