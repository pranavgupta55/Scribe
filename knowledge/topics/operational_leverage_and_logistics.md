# Operational Leverage and Logistics

> Physical products shows/live-sales are profitable per event ($5k-$7k profit each); reinvesting all gross profit into more inventory is a short-term tactic that becomes unsustainable without financial forecasting.

## Claims
- For a luxury goods live-selling business ($6M/year selling designer bags and sunglasses on Whatnot with 110,000 followers, doing 5 hours/day and making $5k-$7k profit per show), the path to $38M is hiring 2 more camera salespeople and training them systematically. — [helping_e_commerce_business_owners_scale_8C_6qojTA78.txt]
- Unless shipping/logistics is a core competitive differentiator (like it is for Amazon), outsourcing to a 3PL or logistics partner is higher-leverage than running an in-house warehouse; the value is in sales/distribution, not cardboard-box optimization. — [helping_e_commerce_business_owners_scale_8C_6qojTA78.txt]
