# Affiliate Program

> The brand has 4,500 TikTok Shop affiliates preparing for the Summer Splash limited edition can launch.

## Claims
- TikTok Shop affiliates earn 15 to 20 percent of the sale price, translating to 4 to 7 dollars per sale for Bloom products depending on the product. — [1b_number_1_tiktok_shop.txt § "How the TikTok Shop Affiliate Model Works"]
- The affiliate model incentivizes high-quality content creation because successful videos automatically trigger paid media amplification and continued per-sale payments. — [1b_number_1_tiktok_shop.txt § "How the TikTok Shop Affiliate Model Works"]
- The brand has 4,500 TikTok Shop affiliates preparing for the Summer Splash limited edition can launch. — [1b_number_1_tiktok_shop.txt § "Summer Splash Affiliate Discord Call"]
- The top three sellers of Summer Splash in the first two weeks from launch will each receive a blue mini fridge. — [1b_number_1_tiktok_shop.txt § "Summer Splash Affiliate Discord Call"]
- Affiliates made over $50,000 personally from selling creatine gummies, with the speaker describing this as a life-changing amount of money and committing to replicate that experience with Summer Splash. — [1b_number_1_tiktok_shop.txt § "Summer Splash Affiliate Discord Call"]
- TikTok Shop affiliates convert sales through a seven-day attribution window into brick and mortar retailers, mirroring how Bloom's traditional influencer program worked on TikTok before TikTok Shop existed. — [1b_number_1_tiktok_shop.txt § "TikTok Shop Summit and CEO Meeting"]
