# Scaling Supply Constraints

> Supply constraint solutions include: (1) raise price/fewer buyers, (2) change delivery ratio (1-on-1 to 1-on-many), (3) productize, (4) hire people. For Couplepreneurs, the 1-on-4 ratio is preferred because it avoids key-person risk while maintaining X-factor quality.
