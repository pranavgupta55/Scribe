# Service Quality and Premium Margins

> Rich customers want three things: fast, easy, guaranteed; they will pay premium for any product/service that saves time, simplifies choice, or guarantees outcome

## Claims
- Rich customers want three things: fast, easy, guaranteed; they will pay premium for any product/service that saves time, simplifies choice, or guarantees outcome — [why_you_aren_t_making_as_much_money_as_you_want_ZuJryiwxjDw.txt]
- To charge premium for service delivery, do pre-work for rich customers (pre-choose options, scout location, drive to door); cost is 10% of price increase but they pay 100% for it — [why_you_aren_t_making_as_much_money_as_you_want_ZuJryiwxjDw.txt]
- Services business must serve rich at premium price; 80% of U.S. businesses are service-based and don't have automation, so mass-market serving is capital-intensive and low-margin — [why_you_aren_t_making_as_much_money_as_you_want_ZuJryiwxjDw.txt]
- Virtuousness cycle in services: high price → high margin → hire better talent → better service → better reputation → word of mouth → higher demand → price increases — [why_you_aren_t_making_as_much_money_as_you_want_ZuJryiwxjDw.txt]
