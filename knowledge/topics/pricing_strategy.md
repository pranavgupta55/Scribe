# Pricing Strategy

> Set expectations at zero and over-deliver, rather than promising the world and under-delivering.

## Claims
- At Gym Launch, charging $16,000 (32x higher than low competitors, 3x higher than previous premium) while doing less work created explosive growth and better results for clients. — [100m_offers.txt]
- The virtuous cycle of price: raising prices increases emotional investment, perceived value, client results, and attracts better clients while improving margins. — [100m_offers.txt]
- A 10% price increase from $1,500 to $1,650 per unit can result in a 25% increase in net profit (roughly $100k+ per year at current volume) as long as close rate stays above 65%. — [building_a_2_500_000_business_for_a_stranger_in_36_minutes_OQf2Ba-Lp_4.txt]
- Raising prices can increase perceived value and customer conviction in service delivery; higher prices signal quality and reduce demandingness of customers. — [building_a_2_500_000_business_for_a_stranger_in_36_minutes_OQf2Ba-Lp_4.txt]
- Corey had an 82% close rate before the consultation, indicating room for price increases since 80%+ close rates signal underpricing. — [building_a_2_500_000_business_for_a_stranger_in_36_minutes_OQf2Ba-Lp_4.txt]
- A 23% price increase after the first workshop resulted in higher close rate and more revenue. — [building_a_2_500_000_business_for_a_stranger_in_36_minutes_OQf2Ba-Lp_4.txt]
- Business has 18-19% net profit margin; trailing 12 months at $2.2M revenue, targeting $3M by year-end — [helping_a_3m_balloon_arch_business_fix_their_offer_lJ6jkoD5ApA.txt]
- 2x markup on subcontractor fees to protect margin; charge higher of advertised price or cost-plus formula — [helping_a_3m_balloon_arch_business_fix_their_offer_lJ6jkoD5ApA.txt]
- The business has tripled prices from year 1 to year 14 but still lacks profitability, indicating a pricing and sales problem, not just cost structure. — [helping_a_concrete_business_owner_fix_his_margins_f0AY76fE9GI.txt]
- The optimal price is not where you close the most deals but where you make the most money, which is past the peak close-rate curve (expect 10% close rate at higher price). — [helping_a_concrete_business_owner_fix_his_margins_f0AY76fE9GI.txt]
- Playing business on defense (spend only enough to cover fixed costs) instead of offense (invest to be rich) — [scale_any_business_with_these_3_levers_3xrL1nQcqlU.txt]
- $500K revenue in 4 months selling digital marketing to SMBs (cleaning, yard work companies); $450/week or $2K/month price point — [smb_clients_will_quietly_bankrupt_you_3Lvhd3LIwwY.txt]
- Pricing principle: sell extremely expensive to a select few (high gross margins) or super cheap to everyone (scalable product). The middle is where people die. — [watch_this_to_get_ahead_in_2026_spXH1kJ6q-Q.txt]
- Serve unscalable businesses upmarket where you get 10x the price. Scalable, mass-market products require capital and scale (e.g., Amazon raised $680B); undercapitalized entrepreneurs shouldn't pursue mass-market low-price. — [watch_this_to_get_ahead_in_2026_spXH1kJ6q-Q.txt]
- Low-price customers have small budgets relative to income/savings, making them extremely needy and difficult to satisfy; their expectations resemble someone giving you their life savings. — [watch_this_to_get_ahead_in_2026_spXH1kJ6q-Q.txt]
- Set expectations at zero and over-deliver, rather than promising the world and under-delivering. — [watch_this_to_get_ahead_in_2026_spXH1kJ6q-Q.txt]
- Adding good friction (qualifications) to a $10k offer increased cost per call from $200 to $500 but ROAS from 3x to 6x, with sales efficiency doubling or tripling — [we_have_more_demand_than_we_can_handle_how_do_i_get_to_10m_lCmdlNNJkBw.txt]
