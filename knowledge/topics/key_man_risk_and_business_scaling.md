# Key Man Risk and Business Scaling

> Pattern recognition takes years to build; you'll hire two underperformers for every one that works, taking 6 months to recognize the gap and replace them.

## Claims
- Every business has key man risk if it relies on any person. The better you are at something, the more key man you become—an asset and a liability simultaneously. — [watch_this_to_get_ahead_in_2026_spXH1kJ6q-Q.txt]
- Don't seek a unicorn employee; bolt together a composite: hire a rhinoceros (for the horn), a white horse (for the body), and add fireflies (for magic). It won't be as good as you, but it'll work. — [watch_this_to_get_ahead_in_2026_spXH1kJ6q-Q.txt]
- Scale zero: if a business needs you to run it, the model doesn't work. If someone half as good as you can run it at lower margins, you sacrifice margin to scale. — [watch_this_to_get_ahead_in_2026_spXH1kJ6q-Q.txt]
- Example: $20M revenue at 50% margin ($10M profit) with heavy involvement could become $40M revenue at 20% margin ($8M profit) with zero involvement—net gain in business value. — [watch_this_to_get_ahead_in_2026_spXH1kJ6q-Q.txt]
- Do time studies: map your weekly activities, feed to GPT asking 'what are the minimum roles to replace 100% of these?', then hire for those roles accepting margin loss. — [watch_this_to_get_ahead_in_2026_spXH1kJ6q-Q.txt]
- Pattern recognition takes years to build; you'll hire two underperformers for every one that works, taking 6 months to recognize the gap and replace them. — [watch_this_to_get_ahead_in_2026_spXH1kJ6q-Q.txt]
