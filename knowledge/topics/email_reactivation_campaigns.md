# Email Reactivation Campaigns

> Email messaging should highlight 'at no cost to you' with bold and underline since people scan email content.

## Claims
- Email reactivation campaigns can drive 20-30% revenue lift; Corey's baseline of sending 1 email per month can expand to 3-6 mini campaigns (2-3 email sequences per theme). — [building_a_2_500_000_business_for_a_stranger_in_36_minutes_OQf2Ba-Lp_4.txt]
- Testing email subject lines like 'I owe you $175' vs. standard messaging can increase open and response rates significantly. — [building_a_2_500_000_business_for_a_stranger_in_36_minutes_OQf2Ba-Lp_4.txt]
- Compelling email angles: savings (showing annual bills and cost reductions), customer case studies (e.g., 'Casey went from $600/month to $150/month'), and allergy/health benefits. — [building_a_2_500_000_business_for_a_stranger_in_36_minutes_OQf2Ba-Lp_4.txt]
- Email messaging should highlight 'at no cost to you' with bold and underline since people scan email content. — [building_a_2_500_000_business_for_a_stranger_in_36_minutes_OQf2Ba-Lp_4.txt]
- Requesting customer utility bills over last 12 months enables ROI calculations (e.g., savings paid back in 18 months) which increases perceived value of the service. — [building_a_2_500_000_business_for_a_stranger_in_36_minutes_OQf2Ba-Lp_4.txt]
