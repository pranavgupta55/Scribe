# Direct Response vs Brand Building

> Direct response e-commerce arbitrage businesses typically cap at $10M-$12M revenue; CAC (customer acquisition cost) increases and gross margins shrink as you scale, creating unsustainable unit economics.

## Claims
- Direct response e-commerce arbitrage businesses typically cap at $10M-$12M revenue; CAC (customer acquisition cost) increases and gross margins shrink as you scale, creating unsustainable unit economics. — [helping_e_commerce_business_owners_scale_8C_6qojTA78.txt]
- Media arbitrage businesses running only on paid ads become vulnerable to dupe products; competitors undercut prices and list on Amazon once they see your success, unless you have patent protection or a strong brand. — [helping_e_commerce_business_owners_scale_8C_6qojTA78.txt]
- Building a real brand requires recruiting affiliates (influencers who believe in the product), not mercenary direct response affiliates; a strong brand allows one product to be sellable to a PE buyer at 50x-500x multiples, whereas a media arbitrage portfolio is not investable. — [helping_e_commerce_business_owners_scale_8C_6qojTA78.txt]
- Salespeople for e-commerce should be evaluated and trained on specific behaviors, not amorphous qualities; instead of saying "have more charisma," say "raise your voice," "talk faster," "shoulders back" so people understand what to replicate. — [helping_e_commerce_business_owners_scale_8C_6qojTA78.txt]
