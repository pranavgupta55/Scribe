# Facebook Ads Optimization

> Running 40+ ad variations (white background, blue background, different images) can reveal the highest-converting creative.

## Claims
- Facebook ads should be designed for mobile (text size of ~18 points minimum) because users view them at phone-size, not desktop; Corey's current ads are unreadable on mobile. — [building_a_2_500_000_business_for_a_stranger_in_36_minutes_OQf2Ba-Lp_4.txt]
- Effective Facebook ad creative contains minimal text; Corey's best-performing element is a picture of him and his wife with 'cleaning experts' text, plus the free 18-point inspection offer. — [building_a_2_500_000_business_for_a_stranger_in_36_minutes_OQf2Ba-Lp_4.txt]
- Running 40+ ad variations (white background, blue background, different images) can reveal the highest-converting creative. — [building_a_2_500_000_business_for_a_stranger_in_36_minutes_OQf2Ba-Lp_4.txt]
- Best-performing organic Instagram posts can be repurposed as ads by appending a 5-second CTA ("get your free 18-point inspection") and achieve strong performance. — [building_a_2_500_000_business_for_a_stranger_in_36_minutes_OQf2Ba-Lp_4.txt]
- Organic posts are the cheapest way to test new ads; algorithm pre-tests content, making good organic performers into good ads. — [building_a_2_500_000_business_for_a_stranger_in_36_minutes_OQf2Ba-Lp_4.txt]
