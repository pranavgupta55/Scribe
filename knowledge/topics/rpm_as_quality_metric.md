# RPM as Quality Metric

> Stop tracking views as primary metric; track ad revenue as leading indicator of business outcomes.

## Claims
- RPM (revenue per 1,000 views) is superior to raw views - it pairs quantity (views) with quality (audience spending power). — [the_3_types_of_content_and_why_only_one_builds_a_business_NTm8BibjUqQ.txt]
- High RPM correlates with high book sales, opt-ins, and business applications - quality audience beats raw views. — [the_3_types_of_content_and_why_only_one_builds_a_business_NTm8BibjUqQ.txt]
- 90 days of edutainment/entertainment content drove 2-3x views but halved ad revenue - wrong audience at scale. — [the_3_types_of_content_and_why_only_one_builds_a_business_NTm8BibjUqQ.txt]
- Stop tracking views as primary metric; track ad revenue as leading indicator of business outcomes. — [the_3_types_of_content_and_why_only_one_builds_a_business_NTm8BibjUqQ.txt]
