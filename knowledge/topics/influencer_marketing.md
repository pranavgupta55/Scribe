# Influencer Marketing

> Influencer marketing only works at scale—one, five, or ten influencers won't reveal what moves the needle, and impact might not come until the thousandth video.

## Claims
- The influencer program has four pillars: paid influencer (CPM-based negotiation at roughly half of Meta CPM), gifting (free products to micro-influencers), TikTok shop affiliates (start with agency then build in-house), and product placement (e.g., a Bloom fridge visible behind choreographed dance videos without requiring acknowledgment). — [168_hrs_decisions_unseen.txt § "Influencer Program Four Pillars"]
- Paid influencer CPM negotiation uses half of Facebook/Meta CPM as the baseline; for example, if comfortable with a $5 CPM for 10,000 views, the speaker offers $50 per video. — [168_hrs_decisions_unseen.txt § "Influencer Program Four Pillars"]
- Validity-based influencer placements (e.g., someone with incredible hair posting about self-development) command a higher CPM premium than reach-focused placements (e.g., dance videos with a Bloom fridge in the background). — [168_hrs_decisions_unseen.txt § "Influencer Program Four Pillars"]
- Gifting to micro-influencers who request free products via DM has become a significant expense; shipping 12-packs of cans is now a major cost despite the lightweight product strategy. — [168_hrs_decisions_unseen.txt § "Influencer Program Four Pillars"]
- Influencer marketing only works at scale—one, five, or ten influencers won't reveal what moves the needle, and impact might not come until the thousandth video. — [168_hrs_decisions_unseen.txt § "Target Pitch and Magnolia-Scale Partnership"]
- Bloom uses Discord to funnel creators into a community where a full-time team hosts weekly video calls and engages with affiliates daily, building relationships that translate to more content and sales. — [168_hrs_decisions_unseen.txt § "TikTok Shop Launch Playbook"]
- Bloom gave its top 200 affiliates the Shirley Temple hydration product 10 days before the general community launch, then coordinated a 24-hour posting window to overwhelm the TikTok algorithm with volume and velocity of content. — [168_hrs_decisions_unseen.txt § "TikTok Shop Launch Playbook"]
