# Pareto Principle and Profit Concentration

> Serving one high-ticket customer with higher service cost usually doesn't cost significantly more to handle than serving 99 customers; but it is far more profitable

## Claims
- Pareto Principle applies to business: 20% of customers generate 80% of profit; within that 20%, 4% (top customers) generate 64% of profit; within that 64%, top 1% customer generates 51% — [why_you_aren_t_making_as_much_money_as_you_want_ZuJryiwxjDw.txt]
- 20% of customers = 80% of profit; next tier within that 80%: 4% of people = 64% of aggregate profit; next tier: top 1% person = 51% of that 64% — [why_you_aren_t_making_as_much_money_as_you_want_ZuJryiwxjDw.txt]
- Serving one high-ticket customer with higher service cost usually doesn't cost significantly more to handle than serving 99 customers; but it is far more profitable — [why_you_aren_t_making_as_much_money_as_you_want_ZuJryiwxjDw.txt]
- If base tier covers overhead, top tier margin (cost $20, price $100, profit $80) is 10:1 ratio vs. base tier (cost $7, price $10, profit $3); top tier drives profitability — [why_you_aren_t_making_as_much_money_as_you_want_ZuJryiwxjDw.txt]
