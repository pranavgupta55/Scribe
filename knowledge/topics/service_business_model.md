# Service Business Model

> 78% of businesses in the United States are service businesses with slow and steady growth curves

## Claims
- 78% of businesses in the United States are service businesses with slow and steady growth curves — [how_to_grow_any_business_faster_masterclass_qsXxckCbci0.txt]
- Service businesses have minimal capital requirements, high cash flow, and high profit margins but are supply-constrained by talent availability and quality — [how_to_grow_any_business_faster_masterclass_qsXxckCbci0.txt]
- Service business conversion requires people at all three stages: attraction (can scale with ads/content), conversion (usually requires person for high-ticket sales), and delivery (requires service personnel) — [how_to_grow_any_business_faster_masterclass_qsXxckCbci0.txt]
- Insurance company (service business model) requires account reps maintaining accounts and constant agent recruitment; high headcount growth model similar to other service businesses — [how_to_grow_any_business_faster_masterclass_qsXxckCbci0.txt]
- Service business payment model example: $100k transaction with $50k delivery cost creates $50k free cashflow repeatable month-to-month, enabling lifestyle business while scaling if hiring outpaces headcount costs — [how_to_grow_any_business_faster_masterclass_qsXxckCbci0.txt]
- Service business founders often become bottleneck because they are best technicians; this double-edged sword creates constant demand for them to solve complex problems, making founder life big and complex — [how_to_grow_any_business_faster_masterclass_qsXxckCbci0.txt]
