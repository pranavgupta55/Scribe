# Sales Training and Behavioral Language

> A salesperson trained as a copy-paste of the founder (from yesterday's version) will eventually underperform; they should focus on selling, not managing people, and the founder should continuously evolve training standards.

## Claims
- A salesperson trained as a copy-paste of the founder (from yesterday's version) will eventually underperform; they should focus on selling, not managing people, and the founder should continuously evolve training standards. — [helping_e_commerce_business_owners_scale_8C_6qojTA78.txt]
- Training salespeople on "have more charisma" is ineffective; instead, describe behaviors: raise your voice, talk faster, shoulders back, more eye contact, smile more—concrete observable actions compress training time and create replicable excellence. — [helping_e_commerce_business_owners_scale_8C_6qojTA78.txt]
- Most companies fail to train people effectively because they use amorphous language ("have higher energy," "be more confident"); the telephone game of interpretation means no one agrees on what behaviors to do, killing training speed and consistency. — [helping_e_commerce_business_owners_scale_8C_6qojTA78.txt]
- The founder's true pinpoint of value creation (for a live-selling business) is training salespeople to sell; everything else (warehouse, packing) is commoditized and should be delegated so founder can focus on sales training. — [helping_e_commerce_business_owners_scale_8C_6qojTA78.txt]
