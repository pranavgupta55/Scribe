# Founder Dependencies and Task Delegation

> Shopify contractors are abundant, not expensive, and good; if you need 20 hours of specialized Shopify work per week, there's a contractor pool ready to serve you.

## Claims
- Founders with tedious, sporadic work (like editing 300 ads for a launch, not 300/week) should use contractor benches (Fiverr, etc.) that can be turned on/off; e.g., hire 15 editors for a 2-week launch, 1 QA person, then scale down. — [helping_e_commerce_business_owners_scale_8C_6qojTA78.txt]
- A junk drawer of founder-only tasks that are niche-specific and low-leverage is solved by documenting tasks and training contractors or VAs; this trades 20 hours of founder time for 2 hours managing the contractor. — [helping_e_commerce_business_owners_scale_8C_6qojTA78.txt]
- Shopify contractors are abundant, not expensive, and good; if you need 20 hours of specialized Shopify work per week, there's a contractor pool ready to serve you. — [helping_e_commerce_business_owners_scale_8C_6qojTA78.txt]
- For a 4-month-old e-commerce company at $3M annual run rate (4 SKUs across 2+ brands), the constraint is not capital spending but operational execution; once new funnel and ops teams kick in, spending can increase dramatically. — [helping_e_commerce_business_owners_scale_8C_6qojTA78.txt]
