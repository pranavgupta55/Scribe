# Belief Systems of Wealthy vs. Poor

> Wealthy parents teach children high-leverage opportunities are the only ones worth pursuing (management consulting, private equity, investment banking); poor people don't know these paths exist

## Claims
- Rich person earning $100K salary sees $100 as 10% of annual wealth; poor person earning $10K sees it as 1% monthly income; poor people are more demanding, less profitable — [why_you_aren_t_making_as_much_money_as_you_want_ZuJryiwxjDw.txt]
- Wealthy parents teach children high-leverage opportunities are the only ones worth pursuing (management consulting, private equity, investment banking); poor people don't know these paths exist — [why_you_aren_t_making_as_much_money_as_you_want_ZuJryiwxjDw.txt]
- Poor people think in terms of cost; rich people think in terms of ROI and value ratio (cost vs. benefit); never pitch price alone to wealthy customers without value context — [why_you_aren_t_making_as_much_money_as_you_want_ZuJryiwxjDw.txt]
- Most businesses fail because they listen to poor people's advice on how to make money; don't ask broke people how to get money—they don't know where it is — [why_you_aren_t_making_as_much_money_as_you_want_ZuJryiwxjDw.txt]
