# Pricing Strategy and Tiered Offers

> Impulse purchase for consumer: ~$500-600; higher-ticket service purchase: $3K-10K; business owner mid-tier service: $2K-3K/month; cheap business service: $400-800/month

## Claims
- Pricing rule of thumb: for every new pricing tier, 5-10X the price and expect 20% of people to buy; each tier should double total revenue — [why_you_aren_t_making_as_much_money_as_you_want_ZuJryiwxjDw.txt]
- Four-tier pricing example: Tier 1 ($10/mo, 800 customers) → Tier 2 ($100/mo, 20% of 1000 = 200 customers) → Tier 3 ($1000/mo, 40 customers) → Tier 4 ($5000-10000/mo, 8 customers) — [why_you_aren_t_making_as_much_money_as_you_want_ZuJryiwxjDw.txt]
- Example: 10 customers at mix of $10/mo (8 customers) + $50/mo (2 customers) = $80 monthly base revenue + $100 from top tier = doubled total revenue — [why_you_aren_t_making_as_much_money_as_you_want_ZuJryiwxjDw.txt]
- One customer at $10K (cost $2K, profit $8K) equals 400 customers at $50 (cost $25, profit $25) in absolute profit; do not underestimate large prices in small quantities — [why_you_aren_t_making_as_much_money_as_you_want_ZuJryiwxjDw.txt]
- Impulse purchase for consumer: ~$500-600; higher-ticket service purchase: $3K-10K; business owner mid-tier service: $2K-3K/month; cheap business service: $400-800/month — [why_you_aren_t_making_as_much_money_as_you_want_ZuJryiwxjDw.txt]
- Advertisement hack for price: offer high-ticket anchor (no one buys it or very few do), then lower tiers look like great deals by comparison; anchor improves close rates on base tier — [why_you_aren_t_making_as_much_money_as_you_want_ZuJryiwxjDw.txt]
- Pricing error: raising $1,000 offer to $1,500 thinking that's a tier jump; correct jump is 5-10X ($5K-10K next tier); market won't perceive $1,500 as different category — [why_you_aren_t_making_as_much_money_as_you_want_ZuJryiwxjDw.txt]
