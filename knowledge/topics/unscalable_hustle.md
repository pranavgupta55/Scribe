# Unscalable Hustle

> Building something special in public increases the chance of being noticed and attracts talent acquisition, investors, and customers without active networking.

## Claims
- Building something special in public increases the chance of being noticed and attracts talent acquisition, investors, and customers without active networking. — [1b_6_decisions_20s.txt § "Skip Networking, Just Build"]
- The action to solve business problems is to build something special, not to network, because building special things will unlock leverage and growth as people come to you. — [1b_6_decisions_20s.txt § "Skip Networking, Just Build"]
- Greg Levecchia used a Bluetooth keyboard attached to his iPhone to DM customers so frequently that his thumbs risked arthritis, because customer acquisition through direct messaging was critical in Mari Fitness's early days. — [1b_6_decisions_20s.txt § "Scale the Unscalable, Skip Optimization"]
- Early entrepreneurs should not optimize for efficiency or profitability; instead they should spend extra hours hustling in DMs, calling manufacturers, and filming content, because these unscalable activities provide the initial rocket thrust to get a business off the ground. — [1b_6_decisions_20s.txt § "Scale the Unscalable, Skip Optimization"]
