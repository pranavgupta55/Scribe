# Sales Timing and Deprivation

> Build perceived deprivation through agitating pain and extending the gap between where someone is and where they want to be.

## Claims
- Sales timing: the right offer at the wrong time is still the wrong offer. Sell at the point of greatest deprivation, not the point of greatest value. — [watch_this_to_get_ahead_in_2026_spXH1kJ6q-Q.txt]
- When someone is full (satisfied), they won't buy more; when they're starving (deprived), they will. When you have high revenue, you don't feel the need for more revenue. — [watch_this_to_get_ahead_in_2026_spXH1kJ6q-Q.txt]
- Build perceived deprivation through agitating pain and extending the gap between where someone is and where they want to be. — [watch_this_to_get_ahead_in_2026_spXH1kJ6q-Q.txt]
- When around people wealthier than you, you feel poor and get motivated to get richer; if you're the richest person you know, you feel no deprivation. — [watch_this_to_get_ahead_in_2026_spXH1kJ6q-Q.txt]
