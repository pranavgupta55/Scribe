# Momentum Over Timing

> Early entrepreneurs need movement and momentum, not perfect timing, perfect confidence, or clarity, to succeed.

- When an entrepreneur takes a loss on one day, they must reset their scoreboard to zero the next morning—not starting negative or carrying forward the previous day's failure—treating each day as new. — [1b_6_decisions_20s.txt § "Don't Let Failure Make You Skittish"]
