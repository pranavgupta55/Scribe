# Entrepreneur Origin Story

## Claims
- Sean McLaren, the speaker's best friend from college, is not the founder of Timepiece Trading but has become a self-made millionaire working there. — [500m_watch_dealer.txt § "Intro to Sean and the Shop"]
- Sean majored in Economics at Drexel University in a five-year program with three co-ops, interning at SAP and Comcast, positioning him for corporate finance before he declined BlackRock and spent six months applying to over 1,000 positions, all denied. — [500m_watch_dealer.txt § "Sean's Origin Story: From JP Morgan to St. Martin"]
- Sean walked out of his third-round JP Morgan interview, deciding outside the building that finance would still be available at 30 or 40, and chose instead to move to St. Martin and pursue watches with Neil. — [500m_watch_dealer.txt § "Sean's Origin Story: From JP Morgan to St. Martin"]
- Sean ran out of money during six months of job searching in New York because rent drained his entire bank account, which forced him to ask Neil about selling watches to pay for living expenses. — [500m_watch_dealer.txt § "Sean's Origin Story: From JP Morgan to St. Martin"]
- The founders left school in 2017 and moved to Miami in February 2019, initially living in a 1,600 square foot apartment with five people (the speaker, Neil, Neil's brother, Neil's girlfriend, and Neil's cousin), with the speaker sleeping on the couch. — [500m_watch_dealer.txt § "Building Timepiece Trading in Miami"]
- Signing a five-year commercial lease for an office was described as 'very scary' and 'would have bankrupted us' because the company had very low revenue at the time, making the lease commitment alone a major financial risk. — [500m_watch_dealer.txt § "Building Timepiece Trading in Miami"]
