# Licensing vs Franchising

> Licensing models make sense only after you can open one location per quarter without your personal involvement, proving team scalability.

## Claims
- Franchising requires capital intensity as justification; a $500K per-location, $75K per-location profit business doesn't justify franchise overhead; licensing at 6-8% top line is better. — [how_to_say_no_to_the_wrong_opportunities_YJy7PL2apUo.txt]
- Licensing models make sense only after you can open one location per quarter without your personal involvement, proving team scalability. — [how_to_say_no_to_the_wrong_opportunities_YJy7PL2apUo.txt]
- Approval criteria for licensing: open one location per quarter with team that's not you and you're not involved at all; at that point, probably won't care anymore. — [how_to_say_no_to_the_wrong_opportunities_YJy7PL2apUo.txt]
