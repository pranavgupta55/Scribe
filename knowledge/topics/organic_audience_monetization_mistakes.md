# Organic Audience Monetization Mistakes

> The constraint of a hair extension wholesale business is acquiring more stylists and increasing margins, not building a SaaS; improving pricing (to increase margins) and running ads/content to attract stylists solves the real constraint.

## Claims
- The major mistake founders with organic audiences make: you grow audience → sell product → make money → think you should make another product for the same audience; within 2-3 years you have 4 mini-businesses competing for the same customer pool instead of growing audience. — [helping_e_commerce_business_owners_scale_8C_6qojTA78.txt]
- The constraint of a hair extension wholesale business is acquiring more stylists and increasing margins, not building a SaaS; improving pricing (to increase margins) and running ads/content to attract stylists solves the real constraint. — [helping_e_commerce_business_owners_scale_8C_6qojTA78.txt]
- For wholesales business with 50% trade accounts, the acquisition path is running ads, making content, and having hunters (salespeople who activate buyer base) + farmers (people who retain customers); this converts stylists because they make more money reselling extensions. — [helping_e_commerce_business_owners_scale_8C_6qojTA78.txt]
