# Service Business Reinvestment Strategy

> Red Bull's space jump (guy jumping from outer space) is a brand investment example with unclear immediate ROI but huge word-of-mouth value; brand spending creates aspirational moments that drive indirect revenue increases.

## Claims
- Service businesses have two primary reinvestment channels: hiring and up-leveling talent, creating a virtuous cycle where better work increases reputation, allowing higher pricing and acquisition of better talent; and brand spending on aspirational moments (not direct response ads). — [helping_e_commerce_business_owners_scale_8C_6qojTA78.txt]
- Red Bull's space jump (guy jumping from outer space) is a brand investment example with unclear immediate ROI but huge word-of-mouth value; brand spending creates aspirational moments that drive indirect revenue increases. — [helping_e_commerce_business_owners_scale_8C_6qojTA78.txt]
- Service business pricing should always increase over time through a virtuous cycle: good work + more demand + supply scarcity = raise prices + invest in better talent + better work = higher reputation = higher prices; if you can't charge more, you aren't better than competitors. — [helping_e_commerce_business_owners_scale_8C_6qojTA78.txt]
