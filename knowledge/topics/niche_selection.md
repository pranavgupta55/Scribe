# Niche Selection

> A minimum search volume of 400 searches/month is required for niche selection, with SEMrush recommended for keyword research.

## Claims
- A minimum search volume of 400 searches/month is required for niche selection, with SEMrush recommended for keyword research. — [1000_day_selling_leads_gmb.txt § "Setting Up Your GMB Profile: City and Niche Selection"]
- Car detailing is recommended as the easiest niche for beginners because listing the business on Google is simple and there are many searches in most cities, though the average job only pays $100-$250, or about $20 per lead. — [1000_day_selling_leads_gmb.txt § "Setting Up Your GMB Profile: City, Niche, and Name"]
- Skilled trades like plumbing, HVAC, and electrical are recommended as an end goal because jobs can be worth thousands of dollars, meaning fewer calls are needed to generate the same revenue compared to car detailing. — [1000_day_selling_leads_gmb.txt § "Setting Up Your GMB Profile: City, Niche, and Name"]
- To verify a good city and niche, use SEMrush's Keyword Overview tool to check for at least 400 searches a month for the target service in that location. — [1000_day_selling_leads_gmb.txt § "Setting Up Your GMB Profile: City, Niche, and Name"]
